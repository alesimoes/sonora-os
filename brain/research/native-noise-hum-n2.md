Not modelled, and why:
- **Flicker (1/f) noise.** No published 12AX7 figure was found that could
  be cited; forum quotes of M. Blencowe's measurements are the lead, and
  the level varies from tube to tube. Below a few kHz a real tube is
  noisier than this model. Follow-up (a) in the backlog: measure the
  owner's tubes, or cite a published figure.
- **Heater hum** at the mains fundamental (heater-cathode leakage and
  capacitance): no citable coupling figure. The model's hum is the
  supply's ripple only, so its series is the even harmonics of the mains
  (100, 200, 300 Hz ... at 50 Hz). Follow-up (b).

## The method

**Noise.** The stage's small-signal circuit at its operating point
(complex nodal analysis, the same matrices as `small_signal_gain`) gives
each source's transfer to the load. `TriodeStage::small_signal_noise`
returns the six contributions (Rleak, Rgrid, Rk, Rplate, Rload, shot).
The processor injects the noise at the stage's input, in volts at vin, so
the drive, the stage's own frequency response and nonlinearity, and auto
level act on it as on the signal:
- the grid leak's thermal current flows into node a, which Cin ties to
  the input: at vin it is i / (jωCin), brown noise, generated by a leaky
  integrator of white noise (corner 0.5 Hz, so it cannot wander);
- every other source is white at the grid or refers to it as white
  across the band; their sum at 1 kHz over the gain there is one white
  source (about 20 nV/√Hz at vin).

The suite holds the result band by band to ngspice, which is where the
approximation would show.

**Hum.** The reservoir's ripple comes from integrating the bridge, C1 and
the filter in time (trapezoidal, 4000 steps per mains period, the diode
by Newton in w = ln(1 + i/Is), which converges monotonically from the
right of the root) to periodic steady state, then taking its harmonics
over one period. From the reservoir on the circuit is linear at ripple
amplitudes: the filter's transfer, loaded by the stage's own supply
admittance, then the stage's B+ to load transfer
(`TriodeStage::small_signal_supply`). The processor plays the harmonics
within 60 dB of the strongest (5 at 50 Hz) from a phasor bank, the same
on both channels (one supply), added at the load.

One finding on the way: the filter's slow mode (about 0.5 s) moves the
reservoir's load, and after 40 settling periods the ripple was still
0.4 % (0.04 dB) off ngspice's. The integration now re-centres the
filter's nodes on the DC their load implies through the first half of the
settling, and agrees with a 400-period run to 1e-6.

**Levels at 0 %.** Nothing is generated or added while a level is 0 % and
not moving: the golden files recorded before this slice pass unchanged.

The band differences scatter with the estimate's own spread (largest in
the narrow low bands: 25-50 Hz bands span 2-4 FFT bins). The suite takes
3 sigma of that spread off each band before the bar, with the credit capped
at 0.25 dB, so a 30 s quick render passes where the raw 39 Hz figure was
0.51 dB (0.26 dB after the credit). The effective bar per band, 48 kHz:
0.75 dB (0.5 + 0.25) from 25 Hz to 200 Hz in both the 30 s quick and 60 s
full render; above that the credit is 3 sigma and falls below the cap as
bins accumulate, to 0.73 dB at 250 Hz, 0.70 dB at 315 Hz and 0.67 dB at 400 Hz in the 60 s
full render, and 0.75 dB at both in the quick render. The uncapped
credit it replaces let about 1 dB pass at 100 Hz and 0.75 dB at 400 Hz in
the quick render. From 400 Hz up every band is within 0.06 dB. A small negative bias of about 0.1 dB from
60 to 300 Hz may be the single white source standing in for the plate and
load resistors' noise; it is inside the bar.

With auto level the noise and hum come out divided by the drive (a hotter
stage has more signal over the same noise): at the default +6 dB, about
-114 dBFS and -128 dBFS. Eighteen instances of noise at 100 % and 0 dB
drive add to about -95.7 dBFS. These are quiet because a single well-fed
preamp stage is quiet: the model makes what the circuit makes. The editor
does not show these figures yet: that needs the N1 telemetry region
(follow-up (c)).

### Determinism, silence, the common checks

- Noise and hum at 100 %: two fresh renders and a render after `reset()`
  are identical to the bit; the two channels' noise correlates at 0.0003
  (independent seeds); their hum is common.
- 0 %: silence in gives at most 3e-14 out, the stage's own residue at
  rest, there before this slice.
- Non-finite input and denormals pass with noise and hum at 100 % as at
  0 %; zipper sweeps of `noise` and `hum`: HF residue -111.3 and -176.3
  dBFS (bar -94).
- `dsp_noise`: the generator's sequence pinned (xoshiro256**, seed 1);
  Gaussian moments over 2^22 samples: mean 1.2e-3, variance 1.0002,
  kurtosis 2.848 (Irwin-Hall n = 8: 2.85), peak 4.21; flat to 0.028 dB in
  16 bands; the hum bank within 7.9e-15 of direct cosines after 60 s
  (largest partial 1e-3); the supply converged in its step to 1.6e-6 and
  its filter transfer exact to 1e-12.
